The Studio Loop

Six stages. The sixth is the one a project cannot copy.

  1. 1

    Map

    The workflow, inputs, decisions, systems and success metric — written down before anything is built.

  2. 2

    Design

    The agent, its tools, its memory, its permissions and its human checkpoints.

  3. 3

    Build

    AI-Suplex configured, and the required integrations wired.

  4. 4

    Pilot

    A controlled run on real cases, with feedback and a defined success metric.

  5. 5

    Launch

    The team trained, operations documented, monitoring set.

  6. 6

    Compound

    Lessons captured, retrieval improved — then the next workflow.

Compound is why this is not a one-off project. A one-off automation can be copied; a system that captures its own lessons and improves its retrieval cannot.

Questions, answered

Do we need technical staff?

No for a managed engagement. Technical teams can self-host or co-manage.

Will it work with our existing tools?

Usually. We assess integrations during the Discovery Sprint and say so plainly if one is out of reach.

Where does our data live?

Local or cloud, decided per engagement — with retention and access boundaries set in writing.

Can humans approve actions?

Yes. Approvals, escalation and auditability are design requirements, not add-ons.

What happens after launch?

A defined optimisation and support retainer, or a clean handover.

Can we start with one workflow?

That is the recommended way to start.

Do you build custom AI models?

Rarely. We configure and orchestrate models, tools, memory and workflows.
