One local vault. Every layer under your control.

Whether you work in the terminal, inside Obsidian, or through a browser window, every tool in the AI-Suplex range operates on the same plain-file vault on your machine — and nothing becomes permanent memory without your approval.

Layer Role in the workflow Human gate & ownership
01 · AI-Suplex 777 Records workspaces, daily tasklists, session outcomes, and candidate lessons via the 3lm memory loop. Plain Markdown files on your disk · MIT open source
02 · Your AI Agents Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, Zed, OpenCode, Hermes, or Oh-My-Pi attach via AGENTS.md and execute the tasklist. Zero vendor lock-in · Switch agents anytime

04 · AI-Suplex Ultra Reasons across your captured vault to map portfolio opportunities, overdue follow-ups, risks, and weekly operating priorities. Every insight links to evidence · You approve

Our approach

“Open the chassis. Sell the harness.”

The framework is free because it is a commodity. We charge for the two things that are not: the vault that compounds across real work, and the intelligence and operational harness built on top of it.

Your vault, your files, your control

Memory lives in plain Markdown files and local SQLite indexes on your machine. Version it with Git, open it in Obsidian, back it up, and keep it forever with zero cloud lock-in.
