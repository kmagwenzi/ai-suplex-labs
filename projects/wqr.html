WQR turns WhatsApp into a 24/7 sales channel for Zimbabwean SMEs — orders, quotes, payments and invoices, handled automatically, in English and Shona.

Start with WQR See how it works

The problem

Zimbabwe has millions of small businesses and millions of WhatsApp users, yet almost none of those businesses answer a customer who messages at 9 PM. There's no website, no chatbot, no night shift. A missed message is a lost sale, and most SMEs have no way to catch it.

What it does

01

Intent Classification

Understands the customer's message and classifies intent (order, question, tracking, handoff)

02

Natural Language Extraction

Extracts multi-item orders from natural language (English or Shona)

03

Live Inventory Pricing

Prices every line from live inventory — one pricing authority, no stale quotes

04

Quote & Payment Links

Generates a quote, then a Paynow / EcoCash payment link

05

Automated Invoicing

Issues a PDF invoice and records the order

06

Continuous Reliability

Runs 24/7, including during load-shedding
