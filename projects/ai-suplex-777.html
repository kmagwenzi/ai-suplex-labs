Frequently asked questions

Do I need a specific AI agent?

No. AI-Suplex 777 is agent-agnostic. Point any file-capable agent — including Zed, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode, Hermes, or Oh-My-Pi — at AGENTS.md and your vault stays identical when you switch tools.

Where is my data stored?

Everything lives in your own local vault as plain Markdown files, tasklists, and local SQLite databases. Your vault, your files, your control.

Does 777 make decisions without asking me?

No. 777 captures session outcomes and proposes candidate lessons, then asks for your approval before promoting anything into permanent memory.

Is the knowledge graph required?

No. The knowledge graph is completely optional. Core value comes from workspace continuity, captured episodes, human-approved lessons, and local maintenance.

How much does AI-Suplex 777 cost?

AI-Suplex 777 is free and open source under the MIT license.

What is the difference between 777 and AI-Suplex Ultra?

777 is the free foundation that captures your work and restores context per workspace. AI-Suplex Ultra is the paid intelligence layer built on top for cross-workspace insight, weekly operating reviews, Knowledge Canvas, and scheduled mission control.
