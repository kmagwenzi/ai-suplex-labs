Questions, answered.

Do I need 777 to use Ultra?

Yes — 777 captures and maintains the workspaces Ultra reasons over. It is free and open source.

Does Ultra replace my AI agent?

No — it works alongside Codex, Claude Code, Hermes, or whichever agent you already run.

Does my data leave my device?

Core Ultra surfaces are deterministic and local-first. Optional AI enrichment is clearly separated and requires your choice.

Does Ultra make decisions for me?

No — Ultra recommends and explains. You approve what becomes durable knowledge or an action.

What is included in the MVP?

Opportunity Mapper, Knowledge Graph, and Deep Ultra 🦸 Mode are shipping now. Skills Evolution, Insight Resonance, and AS Mission Control complete the six surfaces as they land.
