The Trigger

The Volume Wall: When your vault outgrows your memory

“777 gave your AI a desk and a filing cabinet. Ultra gives it a brain.”

Storing sessions in Markdown is table stakes. When your vault grows past 50 sessions, 120 artifacts, and dozens of client threads, you hit the Volume Wall:

  • × You can’t remember what you proved three cycles ago, so you pay tokens to re-derive it.
  • × Finished artifacts sit in directories instead of being cross-sold to waiting clients.
  • × Daily execution turns into repetitive manual steering rather than a self-running rhythm.

Ultra is the Second Me. It installs a headless operator that runs your daily rhythm, organizes your findings into a living wiki, scores your portfolio for revenue, and projects your reasoning onto interactive visual canvases.

The Autonomous Engine

The AS MC Harness • Headless execution loop

A buyer installs Ultra, points it at their vault, and their rhythm runs itself. The AS MC harness runs the operator loop without a human turning the crank.

Headless Operator Loop

Tools/asmc.js manages the lifecycle of your workspace with open, status, and close commands. Enforces strict staleness guards, partitions four concurrent instance threads, and guarantees clean fresh-clone execution.

$ node Tools/asmc.js open --strict

Deterministic & Agent Crons

rituals.sh fires daily, Saturday, and Tuesday maintenance deterministically with zero model hallucinations. Hermes background agents run 5 specialized audit jobs: operator briefs, weekly reviews, outreach scans, and knowledge backups.

$ bash Tools/cron/rituals.sh daily

The Approvals Gate

The human stays firmly in control of the high-stakes decisions. Tools/approvals.js enforces the Hustler gate across 4 buckets: gauntlet gates, lesson promotions, B-Bomb candidates, and superseded decisions before any write.

$ node Tools/approvals.js --review

Discord Brief Delivery

Wake up to your day already planned. Tools/cron/operator-brief.sh delivers formatted briefs directly into your private Discord channel or local gateway with zero token wander and zero missed deadlines.

Delivered to #general • 0 token wander

Run It

How you run Ultra • Four commands

Ultra is packaged as lightweight, zero-dependency Node.js CLI tools and shell runners. No complex background daemons or bloated cloud stacks:

# 1. Install harness in ~10 seconds on any Linux, macOS, or WSL machine
$ bash install.sh

# 2. Open the headless operator loop and inspect mission status
$ node Tools/asmc.js open

# 3. Fire Deep Ultra Mode for gamified boss battle execution
$ node Tools/deep-ultra.js latest --voice operative

# 4. Project your memory into an offline interactive Knowledge Canvas
$ node Tools/project.js --mapping knowledge-canvas
