## ⚔️ PILLAR 1: THE GAUNTLET

**What it is:** Gamification baked into every session. ASCII art headers. Boss battles. Victory screens.

**How it works:**

```
Session Start
    ↓
╔══════════════════════════════╗
║   🦸 MISSION BRIEFING        ║   ← ASCII header auto-generated
║   Tasklist: 4 tasks          ║
║   Energy: ⚡⚡⚡⚡              ║
╚══════════════════════════════╝

Task T001 ──▶ Battle cleared ──▶ Artifact captured
Task T002 ──▶ Battle cleared ──▶ Insight logged
Task T003 ──▶ SUB BOSS (hardest task)
    ↓
╔══════════════════════════════╗
║   👾 SUB BOSS DEFEATED       ║   ← Triggered on designated task
║   Task T003: COMPLETE        ║
║   B-Bomb promoted            ║
╚══════════════════════════════╝

Task T004 ──▶ 🔥 THE FINAL BOSS (last task)
    ↓
╔══════════════════════════════╗
║  🔥 FINAL BOSS OBLITERATED   ║   ← Tasklist complete = victory
║  Mission: SUCCESS            ║
║  Report: FILED               ║
╚══════════════════════════════╝
    ↓
Session End Report → Deep Ultra 🦸 Debrief
```

**Key Features:**
- Every task is a battle. Complete it → move to the next opponent.
- **Sub Boss** = user-designated (hardest or second-hardest task). Triggers special ASCII victory frame on completion.
- **FINAL BOSS** = the last task on the tasklist. Completing it triggers the full victory screen + debrief.
- Session-end debrief with deploy metrics.
- Cycle completion → "ALL CHAMBERS CLEARED" celebration.
- Fully customizable boss names: rename your Sub Boss to anything — "The Paynow Webhook", "Client Presentation", etc.

## 📚 PILLAR 2: LLM WIKI

**What it is:** An Andrej Karpathy-style auto-wiki. Sibling folder to your AI-Suplex vault that continuously structures your work into a browsable knowledge base.

**How it works:**

```
AI-Suplex-Ultra/          LLM-Wiki/              ← Sibling folders
├── Sessions/             ├── Home.md             ← Auto-generated index
├── Artifacts/            ├── Concepts/           ← Extracted concepts
├── B-Bombs/       ──▶    │   ├── CortexMem.md
├── Insights/             │   ├── Graph-RAG.md
│                         │   └── B-Bomb-Pipeline.md
│                         ├── Projects/           ← Per-project pages
│                         │   ├── WQR.md
│                         │   └── Agent-Terminal.md
│                         ├── Decisions/          ← Decision log
│                         │   └── 2026-Q2.md
│                         └── Search.md           ← Semantic search
```

**Key Features:**
- Auto-generates wiki pages from your B-Bombs, insights, and session reports
- Extracts recurring concepts and creates dedicated pages
- Links related ideas across sessions (e.g., "CortexMem" page links to every session where it was discussed)
- Semantic search across your entire work history
- Updates automatically — no manual curation needed
- Exportable as static HTML for sharing

**Why it sells:** Your AI doesn't just help you work — it builds a **living encyclopedia of everything you've figured out.** Six months in, you have a wiki of your expertise.

## 🧩 PILLAR 4: THE PROJECTION LAYER

**What it is:** The applied layer. The AI-Suplex vault is a **typed, linked, memory-compounded corpus** — and a typed corpus can be **projected into any target**. A **mapping** is a declared transform: `input → output`. Same files, many implementations.

> **The insight (2026-10-06):** the LLM Wiki was shallow because it answered *one* question — markdown → HTML. It was a **format**, not a layer. The real asset is that the vault is *structured*, and structure can be projected. The wiki becomes one mapping among many.

**How it works:**

```
AI-Suplex-777 vault (the source)
├── Memory/knowledge-graph.db    (93 entities · 567 typed relationships)
├── Artifacts/ · B-Bombs/        (typed frontmatter, structured sections)
├── Sessions/                    (start → execution → end report)
└── Memory/semantic/             (decisions)
        │
        ├──▶ knowledge-canvas     → interactive typed graph (HTML)
        ├──▶ decision-canvas      → requirement → decision → evidence canvas
        ├──▶ deliverable-render   → branded client deliverable (HTML/PDF)
        └──▶ …any future plug-in
```

**The market — `Mappings/` is the third registry.** Same contract shape as `Skills/` and `Tools/`. A mapping is a **plug-in**: declare `input → transform → output` in `Mappings/manifest.json`, run it with `Tools/project.js`, and it appears in the gallery. Third parties can author mappings without touching the core.

| Registry | Verb | Artifact |
|---|---|---|
| `Skills/` | *know how* | instructions |
| `Tools/` | *do* | actions |
| **`Mappings/`** | ***show*** | **projections** |

**Why it sells — the self-aware moat:**

> *ECC owns breadth. Whiteboard makes **code** understandable. AI-Suplex makes **the agent's own reasoning** understandable — because the thing being projected **is** its memory.*

No competitor has a typed graph of its own decisions to project. Whiteboard must *infer* understanding from code it didn't write; AI-Suplex *renders memory that already exists*. That is self-awareness in the product sense — and the one thing that cannot be copied without rebuilding the vault. **Deep Ultra 🦸 stops being a persona and becomes a property of the system.**

**The launch inventory (3 mappings):**

| # | Mapping | Role |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Knowledge Canvas** | substrate — the raw material already exists |
| 2 | **Decision Canvas** | understanding — the moat's face |
| 3 | **Deliverable / Proof renderer** | conversion — the sales asset |

**Build order:** 1 → 2 → 3.  **Sell order:** 3 → 2 → 1.

**Why it sells:** the buyer doesn't get features — they get a **market**. Every mapping is a new lens on work they already did, and every lens raises the switching cost.

## 🧠 Learnings & Insights
*What did you learn while creating this artifact?*
1. **Ultra is the real product.** Core is the lead magnet. 7-7-7 is the framework. Ultra is the intelligence layer that justifies the entire stack. $99 feels cheap for what it delivers.
2. **The three pillars are mutually reinforcing.** Gauntlet = emotional hook. LLM Wiki = content engine. Graph RAG = technical moat. Together they create a product that's impossible to copy as a bundle.
3. **Load-Shedding Contingency Brain is the Zim killer feature.** Nobody in Silicon Valley is building offline-first recovery planning. This is a genuine market innovation born from local reality.
4. **Karpathy-style wiki is the content flywheel.** Users who build a wiki of their work over 6+ months will never leave — the switching cost is massive.
5. **The Projection Layer is the real moat (2026-10-06).** Wiki was a format; projection is a layer. A typed vault can be mapped into many applied forms, and each mapping is a plug-in. The system renders its own reasoning back to the human — self-awareness as a product property, not a persona.
